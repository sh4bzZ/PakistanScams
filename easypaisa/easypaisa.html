Fake Accidental Payment Refund

A social engineering scam involving phone-number identification, impersonation, and fraudulent refund requests.

How the scam works

The scammer obtains a large collection of phone numbers, either through purchased databases or other sources.

They may then use a mobile payment application to identify the registered name associated with a phone number, for example, by initiating a payment process.

Having obtained both the victim's phone number and associated name, the scammer has information that can make subsequent social engineering attempts more convincing.

The scammer subsequently contacts the victim and addresses them by their actual name. This can create an initial sense of trust or alarm, as the victim may wonder how the caller obtained their personal information.

The caller then claims that they accidentally transferred money to the victim's account or phone number and requests that the victim return the supposed payment to a specified number.

The objective is to exploit the victim's willingness to resolve an apparent financial mistake and persuade them to transfer money without independently verifying the transaction.

Why this technique works

  • Personal information: Knowing the victim's actual name makes the caller appear more credible.
  • Psychological pressure: The victim may feel obligated to return money they believe was mistakenly received.
  • False legitimacy: The caller presents the situation as an innocent financial mistake rather than a fraudulent request.
  • Urgency and confusion: The victim may act quickly without verifying whether a genuine transaction occurred.

How to protect yourself

  • Never return money based solely on a caller's claim.
  • Independently verify incoming transactions using your official payment application's transaction history.
  • Never disclose your PIN, OTP, password, or authentication credentials.
  • Contact the payment provider through its official support channels if you receive a suspicious payment or refund request.
  • Do not transfer money to a different number based on an unsolicited phone call.
